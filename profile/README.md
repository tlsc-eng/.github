## What we build

**Stop paying for every seat.**<br>
Most business tools charge you monthly for every person who logs in. Software you own is paid for once, and it fits your process instead of a vendor's guess at it.

**Automate the hours you can count.**<br>
Re-typing orders, copying between spreadsheets, chasing approvals in chat. We start with the tasks where you already know the weekly hours, because that is where the payback is easy to check.

**Right-size the hosting bill.**<br>
Plenty of small business apps run on infrastructure built for traffic they will never see. We pick the boring, cheap setup and show you the monthly cost before anything gets built.

## How we work

1. **You tell us the problem.** A 30-minute call about what is slow, manual, or expensive right now. If custom software is the wrong answer, we say so on the call.
2. **You get a scope and a fixed price.** The plan, the price, and the delivery date in writing. No hourly billing and no moving estimates.
3. **You see it working every week.** Not a status report. Working software you can click on, every week, so changing your mind stays cheap.
4. **You own it at the end.** Code, servers, domain, and setup notes handed over in your name. Keep us on for maintenance or hand it to anyone else.

## How we operate

> **You own the code and the accounts.**<br>
> The repository, the servers, and the domain are in your name from day one. Not licensed to you. Yours.

> **Nothing only we can read.**<br>
> Ordinary tools, documented setup, no in-house framework that traps you. Any competent developer can pick up what we build.

> **We tell you when we are full.**<br>
> We run a few projects at a time. If we cannot start when you need us, you hear it on the first call, not three weeks in.
